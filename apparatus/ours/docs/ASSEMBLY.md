# Assembly guide

How to build the loco-manipulation test lane, in 12 steps, from the first deck panel to the objects on the counter. Each step has an animation (rendered in MuJoCo from the same model as the drone shots and the robot runs), the parts it uses, and the order of work. Parts lists and screw counts come from the model, so they match the [bill of materials](BOM.md) (materials total **$6,429**, about **$6,944** with tax).

**Whole build in 30 s:** [build_timelapse.mp4](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/videos/build_timelapse.mp4)

## Before you start

- **Tools:** circular saw or miter saw, drill/driver + impact driver (T25 bit, 1/2 in auger bit), 4 ft level, speed square, tape, clamps, caulk gun, hammer (hanger nails), socket set (3/4 in), jig saw (stringers).
- **Safety:** glasses and hearing protection for cutting; two people for walls, the landing and the door.
- **Lumber is nominal:** a 2x4 is 1-1/2 x 3-1/2 in, a 4x4 3-1/2 in square. All lengths below are actual cut lengths.
- **Space:** about 40 x 12 ft of flat, level floor, plus room to roll the gantry.

## 01 Build the deck panels

![Build the deck panels](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/images/assembly/01-deck.gif)

**Cut:** 18 × 2x4 @ 96"; 81 × 2x4 @ 45"; 9 × 3/4 plywood 96" × 48"

**Hardware:** 1064 1-5/8 in deck screws, 348 3 in deck screws

**Do:**

1. Each panel is 4 x 8 ft: cut 2 end rims and 9 joists from 2x4 (lengths below).
2. Stand the rims on edge, 48 in apart; set the joists between them on edge, 12 in on centre, ends flush.
3. Drive 2 x 3 in screws through the rim into the end of every joist. Check the diagonals match (square).
4. Run a bead of subfloor adhesive on every top edge, drop the 3/4 plywood on, flush to the rims.
5. Screw the sheet down with 1-5/8 in screws every 6 in along every joist and rim.
6. Build 9 panels. Set them end to end on a level floor (shim low spots), and screw neighbouring rims together with 3 in screws every 12 in.

**Check:** All 9 panels flat within 1/8 in across the joints; total lane 36 ft x 8 ft.

## 02 Bolt on the lane walls

![Bolt on the lane walls](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/images/assembly/02-walls.gif)

**Cut:** 3 × 2x4 @ 53-1/4"; 48 × 2x4 @ 48"; 3 × 2x4 @ 42-1/4"; 4 × 2x4 @ 35"; 3 × 2x4 @ 33-1/2"; 6 × 4x4 @ 84-1/4"; 30 × 4x4 @ 40-1/4"; 1 × 4x4 @ 36"

**Parts:** gate hinge (2), post base 4x4

**Hardware:** 32 1/2 in carriage bolts + nut + washer, 36 1-5/8 in deck screws, 276 3 in deck screws, 96 6 in structural screws

**Do:**

1. Stand 4x4 posts outside both long sides of the deck, 2 per 4 ft section, tops 36 in above the deck.
2. Bolt each post through the outside joist with 2 carriage bolts (drill 1/2 in, washer + nut inside).
3. Screw three 2x4 rails per section to the inside faces of the posts, tops at 12, 24 and 36 in.
4. At the start end: a fixed rail panel on the back half, a hinged gate on the front half.
5. At section 6 (counter side), hang the side gate.
6. Stain all rails and posts (BEHR ST-533 Cedar Naturaltone, 2 coats).

**Check:** Posts plumb; rails level; gates swing freely and latch.

## 03 Frame and raise the door wall

![Frame and raise the door wall](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/images/assembly/03-door-wall.gif)

**Cut:** 2 × 2x4 @ 96"; 6 × 2x4 @ 92-5/8"; 4 × 2x4 @ 86-5/8"; 2 × 2x4 @ 82-1/2"; 1 × 2x4 @ 30"; 1 × 2x4 @ 28"; 4 × 2x4 @ 11"; 3 × 2x4 @ 4-5/8"; 2 × 2x6 @ 41"; 4 × 7/16 OSB 96" × 48"

**Hardware:** 6 1/2 in carriage bolts + nut + washer, 456 1-5/8 in deck screws, 63 3 in deck screws, 8 6 in structural screws

**Do:**

1. Frame the wall flat on the deck: bottom and top plates, studs 16 in o.c., king + jack studs and a header around the 38 x 82-1/2 in rough opening.
2. Screw the frame together with 3 in screws (2 per stud end).
3. Stand it up at x = 96 in (8 ft from the start), screw the bottom plate to the deck joists.
4. Skin both faces with OSB: 1-5/8 in screws every 6 in on edges, 12 in in the field.
5. Brace each end with two 2x4 A-braces down to deck cleats.

**Check:** Wall plumb both ways; opening square (diagonals equal).

## 04 Hang the self-closing door

![Hang the self-closing door](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/images/assembly/04-door.gif)

**Parts:** casing (2), lever passage set (5), prehung door (5), spring hinge 4 in (3)

**Do:**

1. Set the 36 x 80 prehung unit in the opening; shim behind the hinges and the strike; screw the jambs.
2. Swap the 3 plain hinges for spring hinges; set the tension so the door closes from 90 deg.
3. Fit the lever set at 36 in; trim both faces with casing.

**Check:** Door swings toward the start, closes and latches on its own from any angle.

## 05 Keypad post and panel

![Keypad post and panel](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/images/assembly/05-keypad.gif)

**Cut:** 2 × 1x4 @ 24-1/2"; 2 × 1x4 @ 17"; 2 × 2x4 @ 13-7/8"; 1 × 4x4 @ 66"; 1 × 3/4 plywood 26" × 17"

**Parts:** 5-digit display (2), LED dome button 100 mm (30), post base 4x4, project box

**Hardware:** 2 1/2 in carriage bolts + nut + washer, 16 1-5/8 in deck screws, 8 3 in deck screws, 4 6 in structural screws

**Do:**

1. Screw a post base to the deck (over a joist) at the right of the door; set the 4x4 post.
2. Tie the post to the door wall with two 2x4 ties.
3. Build the plywood panel box; mount 10 dome buttons, the display and the electronics box.

**Check:** Panel does not move when a button is hit hard.

## 06 Task boards: bulbs, buttons, valves

![Task boards: bulbs, buttons, valves](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/images/assembly/06-task-boards.gif)

**Cut:** 4 × 1x4 @ 15"; 4 × 1x4 @ 13-1/2"; 2 × 2x4 @ 28-1/4"; 2 × 2x4 @ 22"; 2 × 2x4 @ 20"; 2 × 2x4 @ 17-3/4"; 1 × 2x4 @ 13"; 4 × 2x4 @ 8-1/4"; 1 × 4x4 @ 62"; 1 × 4x4 @ 57-1/2"; 2 × 3/4 plywood 15" × 15"; 4 × 3/4 plywood 24" × 24"

**Parts:** LED dome button 100 mm (30), ball valve 3/4 in (30), block (10), nipple 3/4 x close (10), pipe strap (8), porcelain lampholder (20), post base 4x4 (2)

**Hardware:** 11 1/2 in carriage bolts + nut + washer, 79 1-5/8 in deck screws, 20 3 in deck screws, 4 6 in structural screws

**Do:**

1. Lightbulb boards: screw the plywood boards to the door wall, lampholders at wall and floor height.
2. Button post: post base + 4x4, kickers to the deck, two boxed boards with 5 buttons each.
3. Valve post: two boards; screw on the painted 2x4 crosses; mount 10 ball valves on close nipples strapped to wood blocks.

**Check:** Every button clicks; every valve turns a full quarter turn without hitting the board.

## 07 Stepovers, crossing ramps, wall obstacle

![Stepovers, crossing ramps, wall obstacle](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/images/assembly/07-terrain.gif)

**Cut:** 2 × 2x4 @ 36"; 3 × 2x4 @ 33"; 20 × 2x8 @ 48"; 2 × 4x4 @ 31-3/8"; 2 × 3/4 plywood 36" × 36"; 8 × 3/4 plywood 48" × 25-1/4"

**Hardware:** 24 LUS28 joist hangers, 506 1-5/8 in deck screws, 23 3 in deck screws, 4 6 in structural screws

**Do:**

1. Screw the two 4x4 stepovers to the deck in a V (6 in structural screws).
2. Cut 2x8 ribs to a 15 deg gable (rise 6-7/16 in over 24 in); 5 ribs per 4 x 4 ft cell, in hangers or screwed through the end ribs.
3. Skin each ramp face with 3/4 plywood; ridges of neighbouring cells cross at 90 deg (pinwheel).
4. Build the small wall obstacle (2x4 frame, plywood both faces) and screw it down.

**Check:** Walk every ramp: no flex, no proud screw heads.

## 08 Build the landing

![Build the landing](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/images/assembly/08-landing.gif)

**Cut:** 3 × 2x4 @ 103"; 3 × 2x4 @ 51"; 6 × 2x8 @ 96"; 18 × 2x8 @ 19-1/2"; 1 × 4x4 @ 84-1/4"; 2 × 4x4 @ 80"; 9 × 4x4 @ 36"; 2 × 7/16 OSB 48" × 44"; 1 × 7/16 OSB 52-1/2" × 43-1/4"; 1 × 7/16 OSB 96" × 44"; 1 × 3/4 plywood 96" × 48"

**Parts:** post base 4x4 (9)

**Hardware:** 10 1/2 in carriage bolts + nut + washer, 44 LUS28 joist hangers, 315 1-5/8 in deck screws, 47 3 in deck screws, 42 6 in structural screws

**Do:**

1. Screw post bases to the deck over joists; set the 4x4 posts.
2. Through-bolt doubled 2x8 beams to the posts.
3. Hang 2x8 joists between the beams, 12 in o.c. (LUS28 hangers, 10 nails each).
4. Glue and screw the 3/4 plywood deck; skin the open sides with OSB.
5. Guard posts and three rails around the open sides.

**Check:** Stand on every corner: no movement. Deck 44 in above the lane deck.

## 09 Build the stairs

![Build the stairs](https://github.com/meetsitaram/loco-manip-apparatus/raw/main/docs/images/assembly/09-stairs.gif)

**Cut:** 5 × 2x10 @ 42"; 4 × 2x12 @ 69"; 2 × 2x4 @ 59-1/8"; 2 × 2x4 @ 58-1/8"; 1 × 4x4 @ 41"; 1 × 7/16 OSB 48" × 44"; 5 × 3/4 plywood 42" × 5-7/8"; 1 × 3/4 plywood 42" × 6-5/8"

**Parts:** post base 4x4

**Hardware:** 81 1-5/8 in deck screws, 37 3 in deck screws, 1 LSCZ stringer connectors

**Do:**

1. Cut 4 stringers from 2x12: 6 risers of 7-5/16 in, 5 treads of 8-3/4 in.
2. Hang the tops on the landing beam with stringer connectors; screw the feet to the deck.
3. Screw 2x10 treads (2 x 3 in per stringer) and plywood risers.
4. Newel posts, handrails and the side skirt.

**Check:** Every tread level; rise varies by less than 3/16 in.

## Things that would not work as modelled

Found while turning the model into a build sequence. Each one is either fixed in the model or called out in the step.

- **Stairs are steeper than code.** The spec flight fits one 4 ft section: 6 risers of 7-5/16 in and 8-3/4 in treads, about 40 deg. Residential code (IRC) asks for a 7-3/4 in rise at most and a 10 in tread at least. The robots are meant to be tested on this stair, so the model keeps the spec. People should use the side gate, not the stairs.
- **Landing guard is 36 in, not 42 in.** Matches the spec pictures. Code asks 42 in for a walking surface more than 30 in up. Fine for robots; add a temporary top rail if people work up there.
- **Landing guard posts were attached to beam end grain.** The first fastener pass put 1/2 in bolts through the guard posts into the ends of the landing beams, which does not work (a bolt cannot go along a beam). The generator now uses 6 in structural screws through the post into the beam end, plus the post base at the bottom.
- **Landing guards were built before the landing.** They came in with the lane walls (step 2), floating in the air. Moved to the end of step 8.
- **Landing posts stand on the lane deck.** Each post carries up to about 400 lb with a robot on the landing. Set every post base over a deck joist, or add 2x4 blocking under the plywood at each post.
- **Lane posts are screwed to a single 2x4 joist.** A 120 kg robot falling into a rail loads the post hard at the deck. Use 2 carriage bolts per post through the outside joist (in the BOM) and add a 2x4 block inside the joist at each post.
- **Gantry beam trolley needs a steel flange.** A beam trolley rides on a steel I-beam flange; the A-frame beam is wood. Either bolt a short steel beam under it, or skip the trolley and hang the safety line from a rated eye bolt through the beam. The nylon rope is not a rated fall-arrest line; for a 120 kg robot use a rated sling or chain hoist (2:1 margin or better) and lock all four casters.
- **Deck panels need a level floor.** The 2x4 joists bear straight on the floor. Shim low spots under the rims, otherwise the panel joints step and robots trip on them.
- **Gates need latches.** The spec shows hinged gates but no latch; a robot leaning on an unlatched gate walks out of the lane. Two post latches are in the BOM.
